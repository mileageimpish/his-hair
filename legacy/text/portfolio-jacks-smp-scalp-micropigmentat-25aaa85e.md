杰克返嚟探訪我同同我分享佢幾個月前做嘅頭皮著色SMP療程。佢以前曾經做過植髮手術，但家己而家唔係太有效，因為佢繼續失去更多頭髮。杰克堅持佢想將SMP療程保持係佢已植髮嘅頭髮範圍內，唔想再往下進行，呢一點我完全同意！希望你享受呢次訪問！

Jack has come back to visit me and to share his 頭皮著色SMP treatment which he had a few months ago. He had a hair transplant in the past, but it’s not working for him now as he has continued to lose more of his existing hair. Jack insisted that he wanted to keep his SMP treatment within the scope of his transplanted hair, so as not to go any lower than that point, which I totally agree with! Enjoy the interview!
