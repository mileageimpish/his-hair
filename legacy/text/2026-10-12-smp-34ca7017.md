你的紋髮旅程 — 第四步：共同規劃療程方案

自然嘅紋髮（SMP）效果，唔係由第一針開始先決定，而係由療程前嘅細心規劃開始。

每位客人嘅年齡、面形、膚色、原有頭髮同期望都唔一樣，所以紋髮方案亦唔應該千篇一律。髮線、色調、密度同造型方向，都需要由客人同經驗豐富嘅紋髮師一齊商討，直到大家對方案都有清晰而一致嘅理解。

## 療程前會一齊決定啲咩？

### 1. 設計適合你嘅髮線

髮線唔係越低越好，亦唔應該只追求過度整齊。設計時需要考慮年齡、面形、五官比例、原有頭髮同未來脫髮發展，令整體效果自然、平衡，而且經得起時間考驗。

### 2. 選擇合適嘅色調

色素並唔係單純選擇「黑色」。我哋會根據膚色、原有髮色、頭髮粗幼同療程位置調整色調，目標係令紋髮（SMP）同現有頭髮自然融合，而唔會顯得突兀。

### 3. 規劃自然嘅密度

密度唔係越深越好。過深或過密可能失去自然感，因此療程會以分階段方式逐步建立，觀察皮膚反應及癒合效果，再作適當調整。

### 4. 共同決定造型方向

有啲客人偏好柔和、低調嘅效果；有啲希望漸變感更明顯；亦有人鍾意較清晰嘅剃短髮造型。柔和、漸變或輪廓分明，並冇一個標準答案，最重要係適合你。

### 5. 建立坦誠而合理嘅期望

專業規劃亦包括坦誠說明紋髮（SMP）可以改善嘅地方、療程限制、所需節數及長遠維護。只有當你完全了解並認同方案，感到安心同有信心之後，療程先會正式開始。

清晰嘅方案，令自然效果由開始已經有方向。

## Planning Your SMP Treatment Together

A natural-looking SMP result does not begin with the first treatment session—it begins with careful planning.

Every client has a different age, facial structure, skin tone, existing hair and personal preference. That is why Scalp Micropigmentation should never follow a one-style-fits-all approach.

### Designing a hairline that suits you

A lower hairline is not necessarily a better hairline. The design should complement your age, facial proportions, existing hair and likely future hair-loss pattern, creating a result that remains balanced and believable.

### Choosing the right pigment tone

Pigment selection is not simply a choice of “black.” Tone is adjusted around your skin complexion, natural hair colour, hair calibre and treatment area so that SMP blends naturally rather than standing apart.

### Planning the appropriate density

Greater density is not always more natural. SMP is built gradually over separate sessions, allowing us to assess skin response and healed results before carefully adding further depth.

### Agreeing on the style

Some clients prefer a soft and understated finish, others a feathered transition, and some a cleaner, more defined shaved look. The direction is decided together according to what genuinely suits you.

### Setting honest expectations

Professional planning includes explaining what SMP may improve, its limitations, the treatment stages and future maintenance. Nothing begins until you understand the plan and feel comfortable moving forward.

A clear plan creates the foundation for a natural result.
