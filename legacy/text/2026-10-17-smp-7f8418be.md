你的紋髮旅程 — 第五步：了解療程過程

自然嘅紋髮（SMP）效果，係一步一步建立出嚟嘅。完成諮詢、頭皮評估同療程規劃之後，實際療程會點樣進行？了解每個階段，可以令你更安心，亦對過程有清楚而合理嘅期望。

## 1. 開始前，再次確認方案

開始前，我會同你再次確認髮線設計、色調同療程範圍，解答你嘅疑問，並講解療程及護理安排。清晰溝通係整個過程嘅一部分。

## 2. 第一節：先建立基礎

紋髮（SMP）透過細緻嘅色素點，營造近似剃短毛囊嘅視覺效果。第一節先建立基礎，並觀察頭皮對療程嘅反應。唔需要急於一次過追求最深、最密嘅效果。

## 3. 分階段調整深淺與密度

之後嘅療程會按皮膚復原後嘅情況，逐步調整深淺、密度同細節，令處理位置同現有頭髮自然融合。每節之間會留時間俾皮膚復原；具體間隔按個人情況安排。

## 4. 復原、覆診與檢視

療程後會提供適合你嘅護理指引。剛完成時嘅色調，並唔等於穩定後嘅最終呈現，所以需要待皮膚復原後再檢視整體效果，討論有冇需要作進一步跟進。

## HIS 報價包括啲咩？

你嘅療程報價包括3節獨立療程及完成後嘅評估覆診。療程範圍、安排及費用會喺到店諮詢或 WhatsApp 視像評估時清楚講解，讓你了解後再決定。

每個人嘅頭皮、現有髮量同期望都唔同。紋髮（SMP）係改善外觀嘅方法，並唔會令頭髮重新生長，亦唔能夠保證每個人有相同效果。

## The SMP Treatment Process in Hong Kong — Part 5

A natural-looking SMP result is built step by step. After consultation, scalp assessment and treatment planning, knowing what happens during each session can help you feel comfortable and set realistic expectations.

### 1. Review the plan together

Before treatment begins, we confirm your hairline design, pigment shade and treatment area, answer your questions, and explain the treatment and aftercare arrangements.

### 2. Establish the foundation

Scalp Micropigmentation uses small pigment impressions to create the visual appearance of closely shaved follicles. The first session establishes a foundation and allows the scalp’s response to be assessed. The aim is a gradual, considered result.

### 3. Build shade and density in stages

Later sessions refine the shade, density and details according to the settled appearance, helping the treated area blend with existing hair. Time is allowed for the skin to recover between sessions, with intervals arranged around individual circumstances.

### 4. Heal, review and follow up

You will receive aftercare guidance appropriate to your treatment. The immediate appearance is not the final settled result. Once the skin has healed, we review the overall appearance and discuss whether any further follow-up is needed.

### What does the HIS quotation include?

Your quotation includes three individual treatment sessions and a post-treatment assessment appointment. The treatment area, arrangements and costs are explained during an in-clinic consultation or WhatsApp video assessment, so you can make an informed decision.

Every scalp, hair-loss pattern and personal goal is different. SMP improves visual appearance; it does not regrow hair, and individual results vary.
