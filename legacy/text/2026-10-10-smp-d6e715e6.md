你的紋髮旅程 — 第三步：頭髮及頭皮評估

每個人嘅脫髮情況都唔一樣。

無論係髮線後移、頭髮稀疏、頭頂脫髮，定係兩側額角後移，雖然都可能適合紋髮（SMP），但所需要嘅處理方法並不相同。療程開始之前，一次仔細而坦誠嘅頭髮及頭皮評估，可以幫助我哋了解你真正需要嘅方案。

## 評估會了解啲咩？

### 1. 脫髮類型及發展程度

我哋會先了解脫髮主要集中喺髮線、額角、頭頂，定係屬於整體瀰漫性稀疏。唔同位置、程度同未來變化，都會影響紋髮（SMP）嘅範圍、密度同設計。

### 2. 頭皮狀況及敏感程度

頭皮嘅乾燥或油脂程度、敏感情況、疤痕及其他皮膚問題，都需要喺療程前仔細觀察。如果頭皮並未處於適合接受療程嘅狀態，我哋會如實建議先處理相關問題，而唔會急於開始。

### 3. 現有頭髮密度及生長方向

自然效果並唔係單靠加入色素，而係要配合現有頭髮嘅密度、粗幼及生長方向。評估可以幫助我哋判斷邊啲位置適合增加視覺密度，以及點樣令新舊部分自然融合。

### 4. 膚色、原有髮色及個人需要

色素選擇要配合膚色同天然髮色；髮線設計亦要考慮面形、年齡、生活習慣同個人期望。目標唔係做出一個人人相同嘅效果，而係規劃一個自然、平衡，而且適合你嘅方案。

### 5. 紋髮（SMP）可以自然融合嘅位置

專業評估唔只係決定邊個位置需要加入色素，而係先了解你現有嘅頭髮同頭皮狀況，再將療程範圍、密度、髮線同長遠維護一併考慮。

合適嘅療程，由準確評估開始。 坦誠建議・絕不硬銷

## Hair & Scalp Assessment Before SMP

No two people experience hair loss in exactly the same way.

A receding hairline, diffuse thinning, crown loss and temple recession may all benefit from Scalp Micropigmentation, but each requires a different treatment plan. Before recommending SMP, we carefully assess:

- Your pattern and progression of hair loss
- The condition and sensitivity of your scalp
- Your remaining hair density and direction
- Your skin tone and natural hair colour
- The areas where SMP can blend most naturally

### Understanding the pattern—not only the empty area

The location and progression of hair loss affect the treatment area, density and design. Hairline recession, crown loss and diffuse thinning each need a different approach.

### Checking the condition of the scalp

We assess texture, dryness or oiliness, sensitivity, scarring and any visible scalp concerns. If the scalp is not ready for treatment, we will explain this honestly and recommend waiting or seeking appropriate advice first.

### Planning around existing hair, colour and direction

Natural-looking SMP needs to work with the hair that is already present. Pigment tone, impression density and placement are planned around your natural colour, growth direction, skin tone and individual features.

### Turning the assessment into a personal plan

A proper assessment is not simply about deciding where to add pigment. It is about understanding what is already there—and planning a result that looks balanced, natural and appropriate for you.

The right treatment begins with the right assessment. Honest advice. No pressure.
