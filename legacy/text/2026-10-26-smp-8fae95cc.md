## Life After SMP: Long-Term Care & Touch-Ups

## 1. 紋髮之後，生活繼續精彩

### Life after SMP

完成紋髮（SMP）之後，生活可以回到簡單嘅日常。返工、見朋友、影相——慢慢習慣自己嘅新形象，安心享受生活。療程完成，跟進關懷繼續。

Life after scalp micropigmentation can settle into a simple everyday routine. Work, time with friends and photographs—getting comfortable with your new appearance and enjoying life. Your treatment is complete. Our support continues.

## 2. 簡單護理，融入日常

### A simple everyday routine

頭皮完全癒合後，保持清潔，按需要保濕。選擇配合效果嘅髮長，讓日常整理更方便。剃短造型同增加視覺密度嘅療程，適合嘅髮長可以唔同；有疑問可以向 Jonathan 查詢。

Once fully healed, keep your scalp clean and moisturise as needed. Choose a hair length that complements your result. A shaved-look treatment and density work may suit different hair lengths; Jonathan can guide you individually.

## 3. 防曬，係長期護理嘅一部分

### Make sun protection a habit

頭皮癒合後，外出可用帽同防曬保護。溫和護理，避免刺激頭皮。初期恢復階段，請先依照你收到嘅個別護理指引。

Once healed, use a hat and sunscreen outdoors. Care gently and avoid irritating your scalp. During the initial recovery period, follow the individual aftercare instructions provided to you.

## 4. 幾時需要補色？

### When is a touch-up needed?

我唔會單憑過咗幾多年，就建議你補色。我會先了解你目前嘅效果、頭皮狀況同想法，再一齊決定有冇需要。留意顏色或整體外觀嘅變化，有疑問就聯絡我作個別評估。

I won’t recommend a touch-up simply because time has passed. We will look at your current result, scalp condition and preferences before deciding together whether one would be useful. If you notice changes in colour or overall appearance, contact me for an individual review.

## 5. 有疑問，隨時搵我哋

### Support beyond treatment

由第一次諮詢，到療程完成，再到往後嘅跟進，我希望你一直知道：有問題，你都可以搵返我。護理、效果或補色，我哋樂意跟進。— Jonathan

From your first consultation to treatment and the years that follow, I want you to know you can always come back to me with questions. We are here for care, results and touch-up questions. — Jonathan
