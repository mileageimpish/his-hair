Baldness is no longer a worry—HIS helps you change your life!

How Many People in Hong Kong Face Baldness Issues? In Hong Kong, baldness has become a reality for many people. According to research, approximately 30% to 40% of men experience varying degrees of hair loss before the age of 30, and this figure rises to as high as 70% among men over 50. Baldness in women is also not to be underestimated, with about 10% to 20% of women encountering hair loss or baldness issues at some point in their lives.

Such hair loss problems may stem from various factors, including genetics, stress, unbalanced diet, and lifestyle habits. As hair gradually becomes thinner, many people feel their confidence eroded, affecting various aspects of their lives. This is an issue that cannot be ignored, as people of any age may develop concerns about their appearance. However, for those facing baldness problems, HIS Hong Kong Scalp Coloring Hair Center offers an innovative solution—SMP® (Scalp Micro Pigmentation) technology. This technique precisely implants pigments into the scalp to simulate the appearance of natural hair, effectively improving scalp blankness caused by hair loss, making your hair look fuller and helping you regain confidence.

Choosing SMP®, you will be able to see improvement effects quickly, and this non-invasive treatment is suitable for all types of hair loss issues. Whether you are dealing with mild hair thinning or already have noticeable baldness, our professional team can provide personalized services to help you find the most suitable solution.

Don’t let baldness become your worry. Contact HIS Hong Kong Scalp Coloring Hair Center today to learn more about SMP®, and let us help you regain your confidence and beauty!
